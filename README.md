## 🤖 AI in My Development Workflow

I use AI as a development accelerator and learning partner—not as a substitute for understanding or validation.

- **Research and planning:** explore unfamiliar concepts, compare approaches, and clarify requirements.
- **Context and prompting:** provide relevant project context and guide AI tools toward specific tasks.
- **Implementation support:** use AI to explore code, draft changes, and investigate errors.
- **Engineering judgment:** review proposed changes, understand the code, and make the final technical decisions.
- **Validation:** test functionality and verify behavior rather than assuming generated code is correct.

I'm also interested in AI agents, LLM applications, MCP, and tool integrations.

> AI can accelerate implementation. Engineering judgment and responsibility remain mine.
