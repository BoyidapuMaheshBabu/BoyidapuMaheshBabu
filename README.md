## 🤖 How I Use AI

I use AI as a development accelerator and learning tool—not as a substitute for understanding or validation.

- **Define the problem:** clarify requirements, constraints, and expected behavior.
- **Research and plan:** compare approaches, explore unfamiliar code, and choose suitable tools.
- **Guide implementation:** provide context, review changes, and investigate errors.
- **Validate the result:** inspect the code, test functionality, and check behavior on real devices.

> **AI can accelerate implementation. Engineering judgment and responsibility remain mine.**
